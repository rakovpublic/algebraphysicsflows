This framework designed for math modelling and analysis. It's based on group theory (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_theory) and my expirience with big data tools. You can read about key concepts and how to use in manual.

Concrete exact algebras are available through `algebra.concrete.ConcreteMathematics`, which implements the existing `IMathToolInitializer`. It registers N, Z, Q, Q(i), H(Q), Boolean, prime fields, composite residue rings, rational vectors, square and rectangular matrices with characteristic/minimal polynomials, rational eigenspaces and diagonalization, affine solution sets, integer vectors and matrices with Smith witnesses and Diophantine solvers, coordinate tensors, exterior algebras, univariate and multivariate polynomials, polynomial maps with exact vector calculus, polynomial differential forms and pullbacks, polynomial cells and rational chains with exact integration, rational functions, symmetric groups, finite sets, finite relations, finite functions, finite categories, finite functors, natural transformations, equivalence witnesses, adjunctions, finite cones/cocones and limits/colimits, samples, probability measures, finite Markov kernels with stationary laws and exact hitting-time calculations, finitely generated abelian-group types with tensor/Hom/Tor/Ext operations, explicit abelian presentations, quotient-element arithmetic and homomorphisms with kernel/image/cokernel maps, and simplicial complexes with constructive integral homology, torsion cycles, bounding chains, vertex-defined simplicial maps, relative complexes, maps of pairs, natural long exact sequence maps, Mayer-Vietoris sequences, maps of ordered covers and natural simplicial excision diagrams, integral cochains, cup products, relative cochains, natural long exact cohomology sequences, cohomological Mayer-Vietoris maps, typed integral and relative chains, connecting cycles, pairing, standard cap products and general relative cap products with explicit target pairs, and nested relative triples and their maps with natural homology/cohomology exact sequences directly in `MathTool` using implementations of the interfaces in `operations`. Explicit integral prism witnesses and finite contiguity paths now execute through the same algebra and flow interfaces, including path concatenation and accumulated chain/cochain actions. Supplied inverse paths now construct checked simplicial homotopy equivalences, with composition, inversion and mutually inverse integral homology/cohomology maps. Compatible dominated-vertex collapses now construct those witnesses automatically and reduce absolute complexes or relative pairs to deterministic strong cores. Bounded pair-isomorphism search supplies decisions, witnesses and complete map lists; absolute strong-type comparison constructs equivalences through isomorphic cores. Barycentric subdivision now retains face dictionaries for absolute complexes and relative pairs, constructs induced maps and last-vertex naturality witnesses, and supplies inverse integral homology/cohomology maps through the same scalar and flat flows.

See [concrete algebras and usage](docs/CONCRETE_ALGEBRAS.md). Same-algebra unary operations now use `IOneOperandOperation` and `performOneOperandOperation(name)` on items and flows. `ICustomMemberOperation` returns the first operand type; `ILeftProjectionOperation` returns the second operand type, wrapped in `IAlgebraItem`.

Run the tests with `mvn -f groupimp/pom.xml test`.

See [implementation status](docs/IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md), [the operation model](docs/MATHEMATICAL_OPERATION_MODEL.md), [coverage](docs/MATHEMATICS_COVERAGE.md), and [maintenance instructions](docs/ADDING_MATHEMATICS.md).

Signed subdivision now acts on integral chains and cochains, with explicit chain/cochain homotopies witnessing the inverse comparison with the last-vertex map. Typed actions retain full absolute or relative contexts and use the existing second-operand wrappers.

Elementary free-face collapses now remove a compatible face/coface pair from an absolute complex or relative pair. Their 32 native operations expose the reduced pair, inclusion, signed integral chain retraction, chain/cochain homotopies, and inverse homology/cohomology maps through the same item and flow APIs.

Checked collapse sequences now compose those steps and perform deterministic greedy reduction. Their 45 operations also provide bounded exhaustive search to an exact labelled target or a point, with decisions, first witnesses and complete flat enumeration. Returned sequences retain intermediate pairs, composite chain/cochain witnesses and inverse integral maps.

`ChainMap` now provides 35 native operations for arbitrary integral maps between simplicial quotient chain complexes. Complete supplied degree matrices are checked against every boundary equation. Addition, scaling, composition, strict inverses, typed chain/cochain actions and induced integral homology/cohomology maps execute through MathTool, with conversions from simplicial maps, collapse retractions and signed subdivisions.

`ChainHomotopy` adds 33 native operations for integral witnesses between chain maps, including bounded Smith solving. It checks every homotopy equation, retains nonzero loops, supports reversal, concatenation, addition and pre/postcomposition, acts on typed chains/cochains, and exposes the equal induced integral maps. Prisms, contiguity paths, collapse sequences and subdivision witnesses convert directly through the existing MathTool interfaces. Three operations decide integral homotopy existence, construct a witness, or return one particular witness plus a complete integer basis for all remaining choices.

`ChainMapSpace` adds 18 native operations for integral chain maps modulo chain homotopy. It computes full map bases and additive homotopy-class groups with torsion, exposes the underlying Hom differentials, and supports class projection and representative maps through scalar and flat MathTool flows. Precomposition and postcomposition now induce homomorphisms of class groups and expose the underlying Hom action matrices, retaining torsion and full endpoint checks.

`ChainMapClass` adds 26 native operations for homotopy classes that retain their full labelled endpoints. Classes can be added, scaled and composed directly, compared with zero or identity, and converted to representative maps or induced homology/cohomology maps. Flat flows expose minimal class generators and complete degreewise maps. Equality retains torsion and checks geometric contexts even when their abstract groups coincide. New inverse operations decide integral chain-homotopy equivalence, construct a reverse chain map and both inverse homotopies, and invert contextual classes under shared computation budgets.
