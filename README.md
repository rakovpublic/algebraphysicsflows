This framework designed for math modelling and analysis. It's based on group theory (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_theory) and my expirience with big data tools. You can read about key concepts and how to use in manual.

Concrete exact algebras are available through `algebra.concrete.ConcreteMathematics`, which implements the existing `IMathToolInitializer`. It registers N, Z, Q, Q(i), iQ, H(Q), Boolean, prime fields, composite residue rings, rational vectors, square and rectangular matrices with characteristic/minimal polynomials, rational eigenspaces and diagonalization, affine solution sets, integer vectors and matrices with Smith witnesses and Diophantine solvers, coordinate tensors, exterior algebras, univariate and multivariate polynomials, polynomial maps with exact vector calculus, polynomial differential forms and pullbacks, polynomial cells and rational chains with exact integration, rational functions, symmetric groups, finite sets, finite relations, finite functions, finite categories, finite functors, natural transformations, equivalence witnesses, adjunctions, finite cones/cocones and limits/colimits, samples, probability measures, finite Markov kernels with stationary laws and exact hitting-time calculations, finitely generated abelian-group types with tensor/Hom/Tor/Ext operations, explicit abelian presentations, quotient-element arithmetic and homomorphisms with kernel/image/cokernel maps, and simplicial complexes with constructive integral homology, torsion cycles, bounding chains, vertex-defined simplicial maps, relative complexes, maps of pairs, natural long exact sequence maps, Mayer-Vietoris sequences, maps of ordered covers and natural simplicial excision diagrams, integral cochains, cup products, relative cochains, natural long exact cohomology sequences, cohomological Mayer-Vietoris maps, typed integral and relative chains, connecting cycles, pairing, standard cap products and general relative cap products with explicit target pairs, and nested relative triples and their maps with natural homology/cohomology exact sequences directly in `MathTool` using implementations of the interfaces in `operations`. Explicit integral prism witnesses and finite contiguity paths now execute through the same algebra and flow interfaces, including path concatenation and accumulated chain/cochain actions. Supplied inverse paths now construct checked simplicial homotopy equivalences, with composition, inversion and mutually inverse integral homology/cohomology maps. Compatible dominated-vertex collapses now construct those witnesses automatically and reduce absolute complexes or relative pairs to deterministic strong cores. Bounded pair-isomorphism search supplies decisions, witnesses and complete map lists; absolute strong-type comparison constructs equivalences through isomorphic cores. Barycentric subdivision now retains face dictionaries for absolute complexes and relative pairs, constructs induced maps and last-vertex naturality witnesses, and supplies inverse integral homology/cohomology maps through the same scalar and flat flows.

Boolean now provides the standard derived lattice operations and exact `0`/`1` transitions to and from native scalar carriers. A reverse `to-boolean` conversion accepts only the exact zero and one of its carrier, so it never applies arbitrary truthiness to a number, polynomial, tensor, or matrix. The pure-imaginary carrier `iQ` represents `a*i`; its Boolean encoding is deliberately `false -> 0i`, `true -> i`, because that additive line has no multiplicative unit.

The rational octonions `O(Q)` register the Cayley product, norm, inverse and both division orders, with exact Q, Q(i), iQ and H(Q) subalgebras, seven-dimensional imaginary vectors, Boolean encoding and 8 by 8 left-multiplication matrices. `DixonZ2(Q)` adds the exact 64-coordinate crossed product `(Q(i) tensor H(Q) tensor O(Q)) semidirect C2`, with its canonical octonion parity action, grading projections, checked maps to and from Q, Q(i), iQ, H(Q), O(Q), and Boolean, 128-dimensional rational vectors and checked 128 by 128 left multiplication matrices.

`HMN(H(Q);Z,sigma,1)` adds the exact rational-function division subring `H(Q)(t)` of a twisted Hahn–Mal'cev–Neumann completion, with ordered group `Z`, `sigma(q)=i*q*i^-1`, `tau=1`, and group generator `X=i*t`. It connects to Q, Q[x], Q(x), Q(i), iQ, H(Q), Boolean, and to the selected H(Q) slices of O(Q) and `DixonZ2(Q)`. The full Dixon algebra remains nonassociative and therefore has no embedding into this associative division ring.

The finite-support carrier `HMN(H(Q);Z^N,sigma,tau)` accepts any positive runtime rank, integer-vector exponents, and a configurable alternating sign-cocycle matrix over `F_2`. The exact product is `(q X^g)(r X^h)=q sigma_g(r) tau(g,h) X^(g+h)` for `sigma_g=Ad(i)^(g[1])`. Its BSM1 mode doubles the first exponent coordinate `g[0]` when that coordinate is absent from the cocycle, mapping odd residue support into the even subgroup; the registered operation checks this condition. The carrier stores finite supports, not arbitrary well-ordered Hahn series.

`CCDRChiralFold` implements the Synthesis v3.9 fold/chirality, SM-D11, P-A30, and CL9 sign-consistency predictions. `StandardModelWeylGeneration` provides the conventional 16-state left-handed hypercharge signature under `Q=T3+Y` and checks linear/cubic traces. These records do not assert a physical map from those fields to Dixon coordinates or a minimal left ideal because the synthesis does not specify either construction.

`Q(zeta_12)` provides exact arithmetic modulo `Phi_12(x)=x^4-x^2+1`, extended-Euclid inverses, and the selected `Q(i)` subfield `i=zeta_12^3`. A precision-controlled `BigDecimal` evaluator supports cyclotomic phases, the CKM Jarlskog invariant, and the Koide ratio. Octonion derivations fixing `l` expose eight exact `su(3)`-type generators on `O(Q)`; quaternion adjoint actions expose three `su(2)` generators. Dixon crossed-product and Witt elements expose complementary idempotents and their left multiplication matrices. These are algebra-level constructions; minimal ideals and physical gauge identifications are not asserted.

See [concrete algebras and usage](docs/CONCRETE_ALGEBRAS.md). Same-algebra unary operations now use `IOneOperandOperation` and `performOneOperandOperation(name)` on items and flows. `ICustomMemberOperation` returns the first operand type; `ILeftProjectionOperation` returns the second operand type, wrapped in `IAlgebraItem`.

Run the tests with `mvn -f groupimp/pom.xml test`.

Signed subdivision now acts on integral chains and cochains, with explicit chain/cochain homotopies witnessing the inverse comparison with the last-vertex map. Typed actions retain full absolute or relative contexts and use the existing second-operand wrappers.

Elementary free-face collapses now remove a compatible face/coface pair from an absolute complex or relative pair. Their 32 native operations expose the reduced pair, inclusion, signed integral chain retraction, chain/cochain homotopies, and inverse homology/cohomology maps through the same item and flow APIs.

Checked collapse sequences now compose those steps and perform deterministic greedy reduction. Their 45 operations also provide bounded exhaustive search to an exact labelled target or a point, with decisions, first witnesses and complete flat enumeration. Returned sequences retain intermediate pairs, composite chain/cochain witnesses and inverse integral maps.

`ChainMap` now provides 36 native operations for arbitrary integral maps between simplicial quotient chain complexes. Complete supplied degree matrices are checked against every boundary equation. Addition, scaling, composition, strict inverses, typed chain/cochain actions and induced integral homology/cohomology maps execute through MathTool, with conversions from simplicial maps, collapse retractions and signed subdivisions.

`ChainHomotopy` adds 33 native operations for integral witnesses between chain maps, including bounded Smith solving. It checks every homotopy equation, retains nonzero loops, supports reversal, concatenation, addition and pre/postcomposition, acts on typed chains/cochains, and exposes the equal induced integral maps. Prisms, contiguity paths, collapse sequences and subdivision witnesses convert directly through the existing MathTool interfaces. Three operations decide integral homotopy existence, construct a witness, or return one particular witness plus a complete integer basis for all remaining choices.

`ChainMapSpace` adds 18 native operations for integral chain maps modulo chain homotopy. It computes full map bases and additive homotopy-class groups with torsion, exposes the underlying Hom differentials, and supports class projection and representative maps through scalar and flat MathTool flows. Precomposition and postcomposition now induce homomorphisms of class groups and expose the underlying Hom action matrices, retaining torsion and full endpoint checks.

`ChainMapClass` adds 26 native operations for homotopy classes that retain their full labelled endpoints. Classes can be added, scaled and composed directly, compared with zero or identity, and converted to representative maps or induced homology/cohomology maps. Flat flows expose minimal class generators and complete degreewise maps. Equality retains torsion and checks geometric contexts even when their abstract groups coincide. New inverse operations decide integral chain-homotopy equivalence, construct a reverse chain map and both inverse homotopies, and invert contextual classes under shared computation budgets.

`ChainEquivalence` adds 36 native operations that retain a forward chain map, its inverse and both inverse homotopies. Supplied bundles are validated against exact endpoints; composition transports their actual witnesses without repeating inverse search. Strict isomorphisms, simplicial equivalences, collapses, collapse sequences and subdivisions now convert directly with their constructed witnesses. Typed chain/cochain actions and inverse-homotopy fillings, inverse integral maps and class projections execute through the existing scalar and flat MathTool flows.

`ChainCone` adds 24 native operations for integral mapping cones of retained chain maps, including signed boundaries, constructive homology/cohomology, inclusion and shifted projection maps, and both long exact sequences. Scalar and flat MathTool flows preserve integral torsion and exact presentations. `ChainMap.is-quasi-isomorphism` decides whether the cone is acyclic without invoking the simultaneous inverse solver.

`ChainConeMap` provides 36 native operations for maps between cones from supplied homotopy-commutative squares. It retains the chosen homotopy, adds and scales parallel maps, composes and inverts compatible squares, and exposes both integral homology/cohomology maps and all four vertical maps between their exact sequences through scalar and flat MathTool flows. Separate predicates distinguish invertibility of both vertical maps from invertibility of the total cone matrices.

`ConeChain` and `ConeCochain` add 53 operations for typed cone coordinates, boundaries/coboundaries, integral classes and generators, typed primitives, structural components and pairing. Cone-map pushforward and pullback use `ILeftProjectionOperation` and return the actual second carrier wrapper. Unary and flat operations compose directly in AlgebraFlow.

`ConeHomotopy` adds 29 native operations for validated integral homotopies between cone maps. It retains actual witnesses, supports concatenation, arithmetic and pre/postcomposition, and acts on typed chains/cochains through the existing wrappers. Cones of strict chain isomorphisms have explicit contractions that fill cycles; scalar and flat flows also expose the equal induced integral maps, including torsion. Bounded integral solving now decides homotopy existence, constructs witnesses and complete affine generators, and builds contractions without requiring strict invertibility of the defining map.

`ConeEquivalence` adds 30 native operations retaining opposite cone maps and both inverse homotopies. Supplied data and strict square isomorphisms construct validated equivalences; composition preserves the chosen witnesses. Typed map and homotopy actions return the actual second carrier wrappers, while flat flows expose both witnesses and mutually inverse integral homology/cohomology maps.

Four operations now solve for a cone map's inverse within the retained-square carrier: a decision, a reverse square, both inverse homotopies, and a complete `ConeEquivalence`. Simultaneous integer equations preserve torsion obstructions and the cone block structure under bounded work.

`ConeMapSpace` adds 12 native operations for integral lattices of retained cone maps and their additive homotopy classes. It computes torsion-aware class groups, full map bases and representative maps, checking that homotopy boundaries belong to the retained-square carrier before forming the quotient. Construction, projection, lifting and flat generators run through the original MathTool and AlgebraFlow APIs.
